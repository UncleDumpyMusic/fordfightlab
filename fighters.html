FIGHTERS

Starter profiles — the lab will keep adding to this wall.

Boxing · Heavyweight

Muhammad Ali

"The Greatest." Three-time heavyweight champion of the world and 1960 Olympic gold medalist. Redefined what a heavyweight could be — speed, footwork, and a jab that scored from everywhere.

  • Pro record: 56–5 (37 KO)
  • Signature: footwork, hand speed, psychological warfare
  • Defining nights: the Frazier trilogy, the Rumble in the Jungle
Boxing · Heavyweight

Mike Tyson

The youngest heavyweight champion in history — 20 years old when he took the WBC title from Trevor Berbick in 1986. Peek-a-boo style, terrifying head movement, and one-punch power in both hands.

  • Signature: peek-a-boo defense into explosive hooks and uppercuts
  • Known for: ending fights in the first round
Boxing · Pound-for-Pound

Sugar Ray Robinson

The reason the phrase "pound-for-pound" exists. Welterweight champion and five-time middleweight champion — most boxing historians still rank him the greatest ever.

  • Signature: flawless combination punching in both directions
  • Legacy: the standard every era's best gets measured against
MMA · Welterweight / Middleweight

Georges St-Pierre

The blueprint for the complete mixed martial artist. Karate base, world-class wrestling he learned as a pro, and a jab that won rounds by itself. Two-division UFC champion, retired 26–2.

  • Signature: takedown timing, jab, game-planning
  • Defining night: returning after four years to win the middleweight title (2017)
MMA · Lightweight

Khabib Nurmagomedov

29–0. Dagestani sambo and wrestling pressure that broke everyone — nobody found the answer before he retired as undefeated UFC lightweight champion in 2020.

  • Signature: chain wrestling, ground-and-pound, cardio for days
  • Known for: making elite fighters carry him on their back for 25 minutes
MMA · Women's GOAT

Amanda Nunes

The Lioness. First woman to hold two UFC titles at the same time — bantamweight and featherweight — with wins over Ronda Rousey, Cris Cyborg, Holly Holm, and Valentina Shevchenko.

  • Signature: right hand, killer instinct, underrated jiu-jitsu
  • Legacy: beat every champion of her era
