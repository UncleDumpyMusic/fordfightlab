FIGHT HISTORY

The nights that changed combat sports.

Boxing · March 8, 1971

Ali vs. Frazier I

"The Fight of the Century." Two undefeated heavyweight champions at Madison Square Garden. Frazier dropped Ali with a left hook in the 15th and won the decision — Ali's first professional loss.

Boxing · October 30, 1974

The Rumble in the Jungle

Ali vs. Foreman in Kinshasa, Zaire. Everyone feared Foreman would hurt him — instead Ali invented the rope-a-dope, let Foreman punch himself empty, and knocked him out in the 8th.

Watch on YouTube
Boxing · October 1, 1975

The Thrilla in Manila

Ali vs. Frazier III. Brutal heat, brutal pace, brutal hatred — Frazier's corner stopped it after 14 rounds. Ali called it the closest thing to death he'd ever felt.

Watch on YouTube
Boxing · April 15, 1985

Hagler vs. Hearns

"The War." Three rounds, no feeling-out, no mercy — many call round one the greatest round in boxing history. Hagler walked through fire and stopped Hearns in the 3rd.

Watch on YouTube
Boxing · February 11, 1990

Tyson vs. Douglas

The biggest upset in sports history. Undefeated, "unbeatable" Tyson met a 42-to-1 underdog in Tokyo who fought the night of his life. Douglas got up from a knockdown and knocked Tyson out in the 10th.

Watch on YouTube
MMA · April 9, 2005

Griffin vs. Bonnar I

The Ultimate Fighter finale — the fight credited with saving the UFC. Two light heavyweights emptied the tank for 15 minutes on live TV; Griffin took the decision, both got contracts, and MMA went mainstream.
